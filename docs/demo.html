One fault, three judges, and only one of them gets it right

Flight hot_gains_lowd — a control-loop limit cycle, captured in SITL on 2026-08-15. Everything below is measured, not illustrated.

What the detectors reported

t = 8.000 fault injected
t = 11.062 actuator_saturation SYMPTOM
t = 12.781 control_oscillation ROOT CAUSE

The cause is reported 1.719 s after the symptom it caused — because the oscillation detector waits two windows before speaking, and the saturation detector does not wait at all. Zero false positives before injection.

So anything that answers “what failed?” by taking the first alarm is wrong here, every single time. That is the trap this flight exists to set.

Where the model deliberately is not

In flight, real time Deterministic detectors. Worst cycle 18 ms of a 1000 ms budget. No model.
After the flight, on the recording The agent reads the captured bundle and names a root cause. This is where the model runs.

No language model is anywhere near flight control, and a test in the suite asserts it. That boundary is the engineering claim, not a limitation: the agent gets the job where being slow is free and being wrong is auditable, and is kept out of the one loop where neither is true.
